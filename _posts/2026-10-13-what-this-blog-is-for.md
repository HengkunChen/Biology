The weekly reflections are short and regular: what I studied, what clicked, what didn't. This blog is for the things that need more room: a longer explanation of a mechanism I finally understood, an open question I can't stop thinking about, a response to a paper, or lessons from learning a technique.

## Ideas for posts

- A long-form explainer of one concept, written for someone outside the field.
- An open question, laid out with what I know and what I'd need to find out.
- A critical read of one paper, beyond the short note in my weekly log.
- What I changed my mind about, and why.

Write these when you have something to say, not on a schedule.
