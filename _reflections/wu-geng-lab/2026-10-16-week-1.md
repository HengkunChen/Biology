## What I studied this week

- Peptide bonds: resonance structures, double bonding, stiffness, planar, trans orientation.
- Secondary structure: alpha helix, beta sheets

### Experimental

- Protein Expression: primer, plasmid, polymerase chain reaction (PCR).
- Protein purification: centrifugation, chromatography, and electrophoresis.

## One idea I understand better now

The distinction between the secondary and tertiary structure lies in where the bonding is happening: in secondary structures, intermolecular bonds occur between molecules on the main chain (primarily the amino and carboxyl groups), whereas in tertiary structure, bonding happens between the side chains (the R group).
