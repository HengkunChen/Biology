## What I studied this week

### Theoretical

- Bispecific antibody:
- Angiogensis: hypoxia, HIF-1α, HIF-1β, VEGF, VEGFR, angioproteins
- Metastasis: epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition (EMT), mesenchymal-to-epithelial transition (MET), TWIST, E-cadherin, β-catenin, fibronectin, vimentin
- Hedgehog: hedgehog (Hh), patched (Ptc), Smoothened (Smo), Sufu, Ci

### Experimental

- Protein-protein interaction detection: Electrophoretic mobility shift assay (EMSA), gel filtration column, co-immunoprecipitation (Co-IP), western blotting, fluorescence detection assay
- Cell growth/migration: wound healing assay, transwell migration assay.
- Transcription activity: luciferase assay
